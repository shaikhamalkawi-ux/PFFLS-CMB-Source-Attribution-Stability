## Email body

Under the Alaska Public Records Act, AS 40.25.100–40.25.295, I request
electronic copies of existing public records maintained or received by the
Alaska Department of Environmental Conservation concerning *The Fairbanks,
Alaska PM2.5 Source Apportionment Research Study* and its related CMB work,
including the winters 2005/2006 through 2012/2013 and Summer 2012. The relevant
period for the records search is January 1, 2005 through December 31, 2014.

Likely custodians or participants include the Division of Air Quality's Air
Non-Point and Mobile Sources Program; Air Monitoring and Quality Assurance
Program; Tony Ward and the University of Montana; OMNI Environmental Services;
RTI; Desert Research Institute; Fairbanks North Star Borough; Sierra Research;
and DEC personnel who received or maintained study deliverables.

### Priority 1 — native archive and inventory

Please provide any existing native electronic archive, delivery package,
directory listing, inventory, manifest, or media image received from the
University of Montana, Tony Ward, OMNI, RTI, DRI, or another contractor for the
study. Native folders or media images are preferred because they preserve the
relationships among input, selector, control, and output files.

### Priority 2 — component records if not already in Priority 1

To the extent not already contained in Priority 1, please provide existing
native records comprising:

1. Every EPA-CMB8.2 ambient/receptor input used for a reported run, including
   sample/site/date/filter identifiers, species concentrations, units, flags,
   blanks, and below-detection or missing-value coding.
2. Corresponding sample- and species-specific uncertainty/error inputs,
   analytical uncertainties, detection-limit terms, blank corrections, and any
   existing uncertainty calculations used by CMB.
3. Every source-profile matrix used or tested, with profile identifiers,
   abundances, profile uncertainties, provenance, version, and any crosswalk
   between report labels and electronic profile IDs. This includes the exact 91
   EPA/SPECIATE/Missoula profiles—especially Missoula Profile 56—and Fairbanks
   OMNI profiles 100–108.
4. Existing CMB input, control, batch, configuration, and run-definition files,
   including `IN*.in8` or functional equivalents; `AD*.sel`, `SP*.sel`, and
   `PR*.sel` selectors or equivalents; species deletion records; fit settings;
   software-version information; and the exact final source/species selector for
   each sample.
5. Native final and preserved trial/sensitivity outputs, logs, residuals,
   databases, spreadsheets, or CSV files, including source contributions,
   standard errors, TSTAT, R-squared, chi-square, percent mass, calculated versus
   measured species, and run-acceptance or rejection flags.
6. Existing crosswalks linking filter/sample identifiers, sites, dates, report
   rows, receptor files, uncertainty files, profiles, controls, outputs, and the
   radiocarbon or levoglucosan subsets.
7. Existing README files, data dictionaries, QA/QC records, checksums, delivery
   manifests, and file inventories needed to interpret or reproduce the package.
8. Contractor-delivered ZIP archives, CD/DVD images or inventories, electronic
   appendices, transmittal attachments, shared-drive folders, or archived project
   directories containing these records, including earlier versions needed to
   interpret final selections.
9. RTI and DRI analytical-result deliveries supplied for CMB use, including
   sample IDs, concentrations, MDLs, uncertainty fields, flags, QA/QC records,
   and delivery manifests.
10. OMNI's existing electronic results for the 41 emissions trials; the
    ADEC/FNSB/Sierra review and selection records; native volume-correction and
    profile-construction files for profiles 100–108; and the numerical hybrid
    wood-smoke profile and derivation supporting `yC,biomass = 0.837`.
11. Contractor transmittals, statements of work, deliverable schedules, and
    Amendments 1–7 only insofar as they identify electronic deliverables,
    custodians, storage locations, or retention requirements.
12. If the package is no longer retained, existing records showing its last
    known custodian/location, transfer, destruction or disposition, applicable
    retention schedule, backup/archive-media inventory, or copies received from
    the organizations named above.

This request is for noncommercial scientific reproducibility and verification.
I am not requesting the final report already available on DEC's website, and I
am not asking DEC to conduct research, perform a new analysis, run a database
query, create a new record, select profiles, calculate missing values, or convert
data into a format it does not maintain.

Please provide records in the native electronic form in which DEC maintains or
received them, preserving original filenames, extensions, folder hierarchy, and
timestamps where practicable. Delivery by secure download or ZIP archive is
preferred. If existing checksums or a delivery manifest exist, please include
them.

This request does not duplicate the separately pending Palmer/Ward request for
the raw levoglucosan/radiocarbon spreadsheet. Please exclude that spreadsheet
unless it is part of an inseparable native archive.

Before incurring any charge exceeding **USD 25**, please provide an
itemized written estimate and await my written authorization. If any portion is
considered an electronic service or product under AS 40.25.115, please identify
that portion separately before performing chargeable work.

If another DEC office maintains the records, please forward this request under
2 AAC 96.320 and notify me of the routing. If clarification would materially
reduce search time or cost, please contact me before proceeding. If records are
withheld, please identify the legal basis and provide reasonably segregable
nonexempt records.
