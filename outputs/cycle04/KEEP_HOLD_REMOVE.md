# Cycle 04 KEEP / HOLD / REMOVE

## Overall decision

**HOLD_WITH_EXACT_BLOCKERS**

Cycle 04 does not recover the exact locked manuscript or independently
recompute the central results. It creates a safe, machine-checked boundary for
independent review.

## KEEP

- The documented accessible-storage search and its explicit scope limit.
- SHA-256 identities for every inventoried historical artifact.
- R3l as the sole historical reconstruction anchor, never as the locked
  baseline.
- The exact numerical and interpretive locks in `LOCKED_RESULTS.json`.
- The claim-to-evidence ledger, including `recomputed_from_raw = no`.
- The minimal R3l-based delta and its provenance labels.
- The fail-closed validator, allowlisted deterministic builder, tests, and
  third-party-file boundary.
- Palmer/Ward and Watson/Chow as live, user-reported pending channels; silence
  or delay is not negative evidence.
- ADEC as `DRAFT ONLY / NOT SENT`.

## HOLD

- The candidate delta until independent review.
- Any full manuscript or Supplement reconstruction.
- Any statement that the central JRC/EPA results were independently reproduced.
- Any claim that the repository provides the complete data/code archive.
- Baseline promotion or a new manuscript version.
- Journal formatting, cover letter, declarations, and submission.
- Repair or reinterpretation of the stale Cycle 01 current-tree manifest until
  archival snapshot semantics are decided explicitly.

## REMOVE

- Any filename or statement that presents R3l, R3i, R3f, R3a, R2, or a
  composite as R3nR7-AE.
- Any untraceable manuscript assembled by cherry-picking historical versions.
- Any assertion that the EPA layer is a field experiment, external validation,
  an accuracy ranking, or evidence of environmental truth.
- Any silent profile, selector, uncertainty, denominator, or source-identity
  substitution.
- Historical manuscript PDFs, third-party raw data, and restricted files from
  the public repository and return ZIP.
- Any claim that a pending request failed or returned no evidence without a
  provider-side record.
