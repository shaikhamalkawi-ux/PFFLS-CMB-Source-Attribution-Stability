# Recent prior-art addendum: endpoint invariance and identification

Checked against primary arXiv pages on2026-09-26. This is an additional novelty boundary, not an endorsement of either preprint's empirical claims.

## Jin and Datta, revised2026

Bora Jin and Abhirup Datta, *Identification and consistent estimation in source apportionment using geometry*, arXiv:2510.03616v3, revised24June2026. [Versioned text](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.03616v3), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.03616).

Sections2.5–3.1 distinguish factor-rescaling invariance from pollutant-unit invariance. Their proposed target is a source-by-pollutant attribution proportion, rather than a raw factor coefficient or a sum across arbitrary measurement units. Sections3.2–3.3 place their identification results under explicit stochastic, geometric and error assumptions. This is prior art against treating scale-invariant attribution or geometry-based source identifiability as a newly discovered general idea.

The present benchmark's compensated82-channel integrated-signal endpoint is invariant to reciprocal factor rescaling. It is nevertheless conditional on the declared channel panel and its weights/units; it must not be relabelled totalPM mass or made invariant to arbitrary channel-wise rescaling by assertion. The two papers address different estimands and settings; the preprint is not a directly executed comparator for the frozen known-profile benchmark.

Only abstract, introduction and relevant methodological sections were inspected for this bounded comparison; no independent validation of its full proofs or algorithm is claimed. Its displayed licence is [arXiv non-exclusive distribution](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html), not a verified permission for us to redistribute the fulltext. Return links and this original short note, not its PDF.

## Consequence for this session

KEEP explicit endpoints, source universes, uncertainty assumptions and reproducible decision witnesses. REMOVE any claim that factor normalization, conservative source grouping, or general identifiability awareness is the new contribution. Any remaining contribution must be supported by a distinct verified CMB-specific result or practical decision benefit, not terminology.
