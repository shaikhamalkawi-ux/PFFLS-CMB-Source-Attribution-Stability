# Scientific disposition of the verified research increment

**Decision: KEEP a qualified, reproducible decision audit; HOLD a stronger
accuracy or method-superiority claim.** This is a synthesis of completed
evidence, not manuscript approval or execution authority. The accepted baseline,
frozen results and published archive are unchanged. The new cold computational
reproduction and independent exact replay now pass; they add no field
observations or environmental validation.

## What has genuinely been added

The contribution is a more explicit separation of four questions: internal
fit, sensitivity to stored profile choices, compatibility under declared
uncertainty, and independently known source accuracy. Exact witnesses and
certificates make the third question checkable without confusing it with the
fourth. This is practical audit infrastructure and a bounded empirical finding,
not demonstrated revolutionary mathematics.

The closest concepts are established: EPA already discusses CMB estimability
and linear-programming solutions; consensus relations across good models,
interval feasibility, dual bounds and solvability radii have prior art.
[EPA protocol](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P1009QZM.TXT),
[Laberge et al.](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/23-0149.html), and
[Hladik–Rohn publication record](https://kam.mff.cuni.cz/~hladik/publ/b2hd-HlaRoh2016a.html)
support these novelty boundaries at the reading depths recorded in the audits.
The original Hougland conference paper and the radius paper's full proofs were
not inspected; no claim of implementation identity follows.

The ordinary comparator is not a weak receptor-error-only baseline. It includes
profile-error terms in EVLS weights and covariance terms in source contrasts,
conditional on fitted weights, pruning and profiles. The released 2025 EVLS
code uses a related plug-in covariance mechanism; its full PMF/DISP pipeline
was not reproduced, and the associated article remains abstract-only.
Joint Bayesian and Monte Carlo approaches are relevant established alternatives,
not executed comparators here. See [methods audit](EVLS_2025_METHODS_AUDIT.md)
and [comparator gaps](BENCHMARK_GAP_SEARCH.md).

## Strongest findings and falsifiers

- **Local largest-source stability is not joint largest-source stability.** The
  3,900-choice retained-source point experiment found joint largest-source
  reversals in two of four samples whose one-at-a-time alternatives were fully
  resolved and whose largest-source identity was stable among basic-screen
  admitted alternatives. This is not stability of the full source ordering.
  Neither central fit passes the stricter mass
  screen. Every sample's complete joint grid contains unresolved fits: 1,102
  choices are unresolved. These are counterexamples within scope, not complete
  grid certificates or strict-screen failures.
- **Much uncertainty is already conventionally visible.** At nominal alpha
  0.05, 25 of 29 basic joint-reversal cases are ordinary non-singletons; four
  overlap the nine conditional singletons. The stricter-screen reversal case
  is not an ordinary singleton. This substantially limits a claim of newly
  detecting uncertainty. Different-scope overlap is not an environmental error
  rate. [Ordinary comparison](ORDINARY_CONTRAST_REPORT.md).
- **Profile and uncertainty assumptions change the answer.** Central full-seven,
  no-mass boxes have 5/33/35 compatible samples and 0/2/0 unique leaders at
  k=1/2/3. Both central k=2 unique decisions are overturned by historical
  alternatives. The original union run remains 34 non-unique plus one HOLD;
  a separate, independently verified, post-hoc zero-LP Farkas completion yields
  one conditional unique and 34 witnessed non-unique unions. The positive case
  accounts for all 120 systems, including 72 incompatible and 48 compatible
  systems. This distinguishes a numerical proof gap from genuine ambiguity;
  neither exact arithmetic nor the small numerical margin establishes physical
  truth or meaningful effect size. [Independent proof review](INTERVAL_FARKAS_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md).
- **Cold regeneration closes a reproducibility gap, not a validation gap.**
  A fresh public-input run and independent no-LP replay regenerate the same
  conditional 34 non-unique/one unique conclusion without historical private
  ledgers. All 4,200 model definitions were reconstructed; witness-based stopping
  required 161 completed components and 1,720 charged LP attempts, not exhaustive
  solves of every component. The fallback was informed by the historical audit;
  this is neither blinded validation nor historical byte reproduction.
  [Cold independent review](COLD_FIELD_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md).
- **The truth control does not establish union benefit.** In all 4,944 rows,
  every nonempty admitted family contains profile 0 only: selected and union
  decisions coincide. Near ties produce 72 errors among 721 point singletons;
  ordinary contrasts report 135 singletons with no observed errors, trading
  reporting rate for reliability. Excluded-truth and underreported-uncertainty
  panels each reject all 768 rows—failures, not successful zero-risk abstention.
  The interval method was not tested. The endpoint is modeled 82-channel signal,
  not total PM or field source truth. All 504 bootstrap intervals were later
  arithmetically replayed; that adds no observations or coverage guarantee.
  [Truth review](TRUTH_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md),
  [later bootstrap check](truth_bootstrap_verification.json).
- **Tracer selection has no established superiority.** Selected versus fixed
  sulfur gives 5/3/0 versus 6/2/0 singleton outcomes among 29 ambiguous cases
  at the three cutoffs. Preserve this mixed/null finding without retuning.

## Practical source-characterization implication

At k=3, all 15 additional lower coefficients for the three available tracers
across five vehicle profiles are zero. Four vehicle profiles already have a
zero lower column over the original species; therefore 96/120 **input systems**
retain it after all three tracers are added. This is not a count of feasible
systems. With nonnegative contributions and independent boxes, that zero column
permits arbitrarily increasing the vehicle contribution **from any feasible
point**. Better ambient precision alone cannot block that direction within a
surviving branch, although added observations may eliminate the branch entirely.
[Verified metadata note](TRACER_RECESSION_VERIFICATION_NOTE.md).

The actionable requirement is a justified new restriction on the source model:
for example, independently supported positive lower source abundance, defensible
profile dependencies, or a physically justified mass upper bound. None is
currently established merely by the archive. A historical mass band is an added
assumption, not new measurement evidence. Bounding a contribution also does not
by itself identify its leader. Do not spend another computation merely appending
these tracers and promise resolution.

## Can the main paper responsibly become stronger?

**Yes, in precision and reproducibility—not in environmental accuracy.** Native
EPA reconstruction strengthens the computational provenance of its existing
sensitivity evidence. A short qualified extension can establish that observed
one-at-a-time largest-source stability need not survive simultaneous substitutions, while the
ordinary comparator explains how much uncertainty was already visible. Those
are genuinely stronger, narrower statements about tested uncertainty scope.

No new result independently validates the JRC 9/30 point-reference comparison,
its reference provenance or aggregation level. Do not turn the EPA sensitivity
counts into error probabilities, identify the environmentally correct profile,
or claim a universally improved estimator. Recommended integration is at most
one concise scope paragraph and a conventional-comparator qualification; keep
the interval/truth work in a separately labelled exploratory supplement or
research package unless it answers a specific main-paper question. Baseline and
disclosure changes require author review. [Integration gate](MANUSCRIPT_INTEGRATION_GATE.md).

## Smallest genuinely decisive next experiment — proposal only

A **blinded, independently prepared known-mass two-source mixture test** is a
smaller decisive step than another broad simulation or radius sweep. Select a
scientifically relevant confounded pair before seeing test outcomes; declare
the particle fraction and mass endpoint. It would test only that pair, not
validate the full seven-family field problem.

First characterize independent source batches with replicate assays, preserving
cross-species dependence and uncertainty provenance. Freeze admissible profiles,
uncertainty rules, assay costs, margins and labels using calibration material
only. Include near-tied mixtures and a separately declared outside-family
challenge. Fix replication by a prospective precision calculation; do not select
mixtures or stop sampling after observing favorable performance.

Compare the conditional audit with EVLS joint contrasts and the strong union
of those contrasts using equal inputs and a predeclared matched-reporting rule.
Report wrong-singleton risk, all-sample reporting rate, truth-in-set coverage,
set size, incompatibility and numerical failure; zero reports give undefined
risk. A gain must persist on independent test batches, not result merely from
more abstention or privileged profile information. If uncertainty sets miss the
known mixtures, all methods reject, or the conventional comparator matches the
decision benefit, stop the superiority claim. This would establish at most a
controlled proof of principle; environmental transfer remains a separate test.
No acquisition, laboratory coordination or execution is authorized here.
