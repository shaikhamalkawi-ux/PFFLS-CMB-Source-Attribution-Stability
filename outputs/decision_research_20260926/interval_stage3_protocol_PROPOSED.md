# PROPOSED targeted Stage 3: lazy proof over the historical profile-system union

**NOT APPROVED FOR EXECUTION. No new Stage 3 LP has been run.**

This is a post-Stage2, targeted falsification/closure proposal. It is not a blinded
experiment, a new uncertainty-scale search, or a prevalence estimate based on four
representative samples. Root must read and approve it, the complete Stage2 proof
validator must pass, and the work must still offer scientific value beyond
ordinary sensitivity methods before execution is considered.

## Fixed scientific scope

- Primary source universe: the original **seven** families, no fit-dependent
  removal; no retained-universe bridge in Stage 3.
- **k=2**, joint receptor/profile intervals, **no mass constraint**. No new k,
  diagnostic screen, species, profile normalization, covariance, or uncertainty
  scaling. This targeted k choice audits the two remaining central-system
  certificates; it is not justified by a claim of probabilistic coverage.
- All original 35 samples remain in the final accounting.
- Profile-system universe: the same exact 120 Cartesian combinations of the
  historical soil(4), wood(3), oil(2), and vehicle(5) alternatives, with AMSUL,
  AMNIT and NANO3 fixed. Profile identity and its **own** released uncertainty
  fields move together. Never substitute an alternative mean while retaining
  central-profile uncertainties.
- The per-system compatible set is the same independent, nonnegative box model
  as Stage2. The union is over actual enumerated systems, not an entrywise envelope
  across alternatives. No environmental truth or simultaneous physical
  plausibility of every historical combination is inferred.

## Why only four samples require new profile-system solves

For the pinned central-system k2/no-mass/full-seven panel, Stage2 has:

- **31** samples with exactly verified feasible co-leader witnesses for at least
  two distinct source families;
- **2** compatible samples with a verified unique leader above the fixed margin
  threshold;
- **2** exactly incompatible central-system samples.

The central system is a member of the historical 120-system union. Therefore the
31 multiple-co-leader witnesses persist in the larger union: enlarging a set
cannot restore a guaranteed strict unique leader once two co-leaders are
possible. Those 31 samples need **no new solve to establish that particular
negative conclusion**. Their complete union contribution ranges, feasible-system
counts, and full possible-leader sets nevertheless remain uncomputed.

Select the four remaining samples solely by the above predicates in the pinned
index. Require the exact 31/2/2 partition before proceeding. This is proof-based
early termination for an all35-sample decision question, not choosing four
representative or favorable dates.

## Deterministic lazy union algorithm

1. Reuse the verified central-system proof records; do not refit them. Traverse
   the other 119 systems in the fixed Cartesian order already declared by the
   grid configuration, with the central tuple explicitly skipped after reuse.
2. For each system, establish exact compatibility or exact Farkas infeasibility
   using the reviewed rational-verification procedures. A numeric failure stays
   unresolved. Do not reject a system because its old EVLS fit failed.
3. For compatible systems, seek exactly feasible co-leader witnesses using all
   leader inequalities jointly. Maintain the union of **witnessed** possible
   co-leader labels. Once it contains two distinct labels, the sample's absence
   of a guaranteed unique union leader is proved; stop further systems for that
   sample. This reports a lower bound on the possible-leader set, not its full
   enumeration.
4. For a potential surviving leader W, obtain verified lower bounds on W minus
   each of the other six contributions. A positive relevant one-sided bound
   suffices; the opposite side need not be bounded. Use the same fixed
   `1e-7*max(1,TMAC)` reporting threshold and retain near-zero HOLD states.
5. A **union-wide unique-leader certificate** is allowed only after every one
   of the 120 systems is either exactly infeasible or has the same W certified
   above that margin against all competitors, and at least one system is exactly
   compatible. No unresolved system or margin may be ignored.
6. An **empty-union certificate** requires exactly verified infeasibility of
   all 120 systems. Otherwise a failure to find a feasible system is only HOLD.
7. If a sample reaches the computational/time budget without either a valid
   ambiguity witness or a complete certificate, retain a clearly labeled
   incomplete-union/HOLD status. Never count it as stable, accurate or empty.

Exact feasible points and rays may be used constructively without new solves,
but any derived witness must satisfy the selected system's original rational
constraints. The proof is conditional on a per-sample latent profile; it does
not establish a single shared latent profile across all35 samples.

## Bounded computation and required outputs

Maximum new profile-system models: **4 x 119 = 476**. Maximum numerical LP calls:
**20,000**, including primary solves, cross-checks, phase-I/ray helpers and
verification auxiliaries. Maximum execution window: **two hours**, and never
beyond the root's final-QA cutoff. Stop at the first limit and report incomplete
coverage. No automatic budget extension or additional dataset/cycle.

The candidate implementation must count every numerical solver invocation and
record exact attempted/skipped/early-stopped system identities. Source hulls and
all pairwise rankings are unnecessary for the specific leader question and are
not silently added. Tests must cover inherited ambiguity, a unique leader
surviving a complete union, reversal in a late system, all systems infeasible,
unresolved systems preventing a positive certificate, and early-stopped results
not being mislabeled exhaustive profile statistics.

Public output: aggregate all35 decision accounting, coverage and stopping counts,
hashes, proof logic, and KEEP/HOLD/REMOVE. Private output: every new matrix,
profile tuple, rational proof/witness, selected sample identity, and inherited
proof linkage. No paper baseline, GitHub merge, raw-data publication, Zenodo
replacement, outreach or external write is part of this proposal.

**KEEP** valid witnessed non-identification and complete conditional certificates.
**HOLD** unfinished unions, physical box plausibility, probability coverage and
accuracy. **REMOVE** “new theorem,” “all120 systems were characterized for every
sample,” or any interpretation that a mass assumption or uncertainty multiplier
is newly acquired evidence.
