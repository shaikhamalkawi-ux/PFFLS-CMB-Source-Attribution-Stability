## Privacy and redistribution boundary

No credentials, account identifiers, personal contact details, absolute owner
workspace paths, native sample vectors, observed sample-date lists, or native
sample-level proof records were found. Public site/profile selectors, species
names and archive filenames are present intentionally; these are method/input
identifiers, not a list of actual receptor-row identities. Relative private
directory names describe excluded outputs. A generic absolute path in an
adversarial path test is a synthetic rejection fixture, not an owner path.

The tests construct artificial records, constant decimal fields, deliberately
fabricated date tokens, and small rational toy proofs. They are not pasted
native rows or excerpts of the actual private proof ledgers. The full-adapter
fixture explicitly stubs original-file authentication/repeated construction;
the tests do not disguise that boundary as a real-archive field replay.

The QA JSON contains counts, aggregate order/model digests and an 18-entry
public dependency map, not its sample lists or matrices. Hashes identify
provenance; they do not distribute the corresponding private contents. The
tracer output supplies aggregate sign/count results only, without the original
coefficient or receptor arrays. Referenced dependency contents outside these
14 files were not newly relicensed or brought into this review's release scope.

No blanket right to redistribute original EPA archives, third-party source
code, articles, or private materials is asserted. The historical-methods note
limits its reading to the official protocol and publisher abstract/metadata,
states that the original conference paper was not inspected, and includes no
fulltext. Its revised approximate/model-dependent wording is appropriately
limited. Apply only the existing manifest-scoped original-code/documentation
licences; input hashes or public accessibility alone are not a new licence.
