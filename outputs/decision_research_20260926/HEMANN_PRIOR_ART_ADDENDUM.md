# Hemann 2009: prior-art addendum

2026-09-27 UTC. Additive literature clarification; no new experiment.

[Hemann et al. (2009), DOI 10.5194/acp-9-497-2009](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-9-497-2009) already evaluates source-contribution bias against known synthetic truth. Its [primary full text](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/9/497/2009/acp-9-497-2009.pdf), section 2.2 (pp. 499-500), specifies 365 days, 39 species and nine sources, with prescribed contributions/profiles, measurement noise and detection-limit replacement. Sections 2.3.1 and 2.6 describe 1,000 four-day circular-block bootstrap analysis replicates, PMF refitting and neural-network factor alignment. Table 4 (p. 507) retains 736 nine-factor replicates after one convergence failure and 263 matching failures.

Sections 4.1-4.2 (pp. 507-510) demonstrate that low bootstrap variability can coexist with substantial contribution bias. Thus, stability-versus-accuracy and known-truth synthetic evaluation are established precedents, not first findings of this candidate.

PMF refits profiles and contributions, unlike our fixed-profile, fixed-effective-weight conditional contrasts. Its daily contribution-bias endpoint differs from our finite-family leadership decisions and 82-channel signal benchmark. Contribution-level bootstrap comparators merit future consideration where the sampling design permits; none was run here. This precedent supplies neither field validation nor a superiority claim.
