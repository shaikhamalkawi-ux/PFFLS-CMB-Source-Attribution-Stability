# Cycle 03 KEEP / HOLD / REMOVE

## Overall decision

**HOLD — zero candidates pass all ten admission gates, and no manuscript change is authorized.**

## KEEP as recovered evidence

- The archived 1 March 2013 Fairbanks report is a new, identity-verified primary source. Its Table 12 contains 39 exact site/date radiocarbon rows from winter 2011/2012; 37 also report a CMB wood-smoke percentage.
- The Fairbanks Table 12 transcription is retained as a same-date published-system crosswalk. Among the 37 reported CMB rows, the CMB percentage is above the published radiocarbon-derived interval on 32 rows, inside it on 4, and below it on 1. These are descriptive checks of the published systems, not a profile ranking.
- Barrow Tables S1 and S2 yield four exact sampling-interval matches between radiocarbon and CMB fossil/contemporary OC and EC estimates, with uncertainties on both sides. Filter identifiers are not published, so this is an exact interval—not proven filter-ID—crosswalk.
- The APHH-Beijing public workbook provides 25 exact dated radiocarbon/extended-Gelencsér records: 13 at IAP and 12 at PG.
- The Chengdu public workbook provides a partial 64-day organic-marker matrix. The study remains useful context showing sensitivity to coal and gasoline profiles and the difference between profile choice and a marker/source-system change.
- All nine locally audited public source files have recorded SHA-256 and MD5 identities; repository-record MD5 values matched where provided.

## HOLD as external-validation candidates

### Fairbanks 2011/2012

The exact-date CMB–radiocarbon table is stronger than the previously public seasonal comparison. It still lacks the historical receptor vectors, per-species uncertainties, complete source-profile vectors and uncertainties, exact per-run source/species selectors, and reproducible control files. The radiocarbon-to-wood-smoke conversion also uses a carbon fraction of 0.837 from an OMNI hybrid wood profile while the compared CMB result explicitly did not use OMNI profiles. The published comparison therefore cannot identify a fit-favored versus externally closest same-family profile.

### Barrow 2012/2013

The same-interval crosswalk is exact for four intervals, but the public record lacks filter IDs, the complete CMB receptor matrix and uncertainties, final source vectors and profile uncertainties, fitted-species list, and run files. More importantly, the radiocarbon-derived contemporary EC result was used in the levoglucosan-lifetime calculations across seven wood ratios before the Maine profile was chosen for CMB. That is external-reference leakage, not a frozen prespecified profile landscape. CMB closes EC approximately, but its resolved primary OC accounts for only about 23–54% of total radiocarbon OC, so the OC endpoint mapping is not equivalent.

### APHH-Beijing

The 25 radiocarbon dates are public, and the RSC comparison explicitly restricts its CMB results to those same 25 samples. The final species/source selector is also reported. However, the CMB values are published only as four seasonal/site group means (`n=7, 6, 7, 5`), not as daily values. No complete sample-level receptor/error/profile/control/output package or frozen same-family alternatives were recovered, so a per-sample external-error calculation remains impossible.

## REMOVE from the target external-validation set

### Chengdu 2018

Remove Chengdu as a candidate for independent profile-resolved external validation. Its EC-tracer SOA comparison is derived from the same OC/EC chemistry using a fixed primary OC/EC ratio of 2.3 and is not an independent same-sample source-specific reference for the coal/gasoline profile tests. The open workbook is only a partial receptor matrix, and OM-CMB versus IOM-CMB changes the marker/source system. Retain the prespecified gasoline-profile landscape and reported 1–8% gasoline range only as contextual profile-sensitivity evidence.

## REMOVE as interpretations

- Do not call Fairbanks Table 12 validation of any individual wood profile.
- Do not turn the 32/37 Fairbanks above-interval count into a claim about which profile is environmentally correct.
- Do not call Barrow's seven levoglucosan/EC ratios seven frozen CMB alternatives; they were used in a radiocarbon-informed lifetime sensitivity calculation.
- Do not treat APHH seasonal group means as sample-level CMB observations.
- Do not treat Chengdu OM-CMB versus IOM-CMB as an isolated profile-choice experiment.
- Do not treat either already-sent external request as failed evidence while a response is pending.
