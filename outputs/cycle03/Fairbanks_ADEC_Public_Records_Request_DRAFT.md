> Under the Alaska Public Records Act, I request electronic copies of existing records held by the Alaska Department of Environmental Conservation that constitute, document, index, or preserve the native electronic analysis package for the ADEC-funded University of Montana / Center for Environmental Health Sciences project *The Fairbanks, Alaska PM2.5 Source Apportionment Research Study*, PI Tony J. Ward. The scope covers the reports delivered in 2010, 2012, and 2013, including the March 1, 2013 and December 2013 versions and Amendments 6 and 7. The University of Montana internal locator is **Fund 328961**; I do not represent that identifier as an ADEC contract number. The relevant sampling years include the winters of 2005/2006 through 2011/2012 and the State Building, North Pole, Peger Road, RAMS, NCORE, and NPF3 analyses described in those reports.

> Please search the Division of Air Quality, including the Air Monitoring & Quality Assurance Program and any project, contract, records-management, network-drive, removable-media, archived-email-attachment, or contractor-deliverable locations reasonably likely to contain the project package. I request the existing native files and folder structure, not a new analysis.

## Why each record class matters

| Record class | Scientific purpose |
|---|---|
| Receptor values and uncertainties | Reconstruct the exact effective-variance objective without imputing uncertainty. |
| Source profiles and uncertainties | Distinguish source-profile choice from source-universe or species-selection changes. |
| Final selectors and deletion logs | Prevent a post-hoc reconstruction from being mislabeled as the historical run. |
| Control files and outputs | Reproduce the published control before testing alternatives. |
| Run/sample crosswalk | Join the exact CMB run to the same filter/date independent reference. |
| Native archive and manifests | Preserve provenance, versions, filenames, and folder relationships. |
| CMB-side run/sample crosswalk | Resolve the exact-date 2011/2012 lead without duplicating the separately pending investigator spreadsheet request. |
