# Cycle 01 KEEP / HOLD / REMOVE

## KEEP

- The exact six NFRAQS hardwood-woodstove profiles, each with 121 abundance and
  one-sigma uncertainty pairs and no imputation.
- The clean-room Welby control reproduction and the locked fit-only finding that
  NWSOWHL is favored among the six candidates under the specified fit objective.
- The prior conclusion that this is **fit evidence only**.
- The Fairbanks report as a documented near-case and provenance lead, including
  its numerical profile appendices and explicit statement that combinations
  were tried until an optimal fit was found.
- The school-bus dual-tracer analysis as contextual evidence that a prespecified
  modeling/selection method can be checked against a source-specific external
  reference on the same runs.
- The fail-closed eight-object admission protocol, source inventory, hashes,
  retrieval script, validator, and tests in this return.

## HOLD

- NFRAQS profile-resolved radiocarbon error, because the historical CMB-side
  `fM` transformation is not defined.
- Any assertion that NWSOWHL is externally more accurate.
- Libby profile-resolved reconstruction, because its public tables omit the
  daily inputs, numerical profiles, uncertainties, and exact selectors.
- Fairbanks profile-resolved reconstruction, because receptor input matrices
  and exact sample-level final selectors are absent and the reported search was
  outcome-guided.
- The school-bus study as an answer to the paper's profile-choice question,
  because it tests species selection and lacks the full numerical input matrix.
- Tennessee Valley and Shenzhen as profile-resolved tests until complete
  sample-level numerical packages and frozen selector/crosswalk records are
  recovered.
- Any new manuscript baseline or revision arising from Cycle 01.

## REMOVE

- Fresno Supersite from the Cycle 01 admission set because the located study has
  no same-sample independent source-discriminating endpoint.
- Treating study-average agreement, correlation, fit diagnostics, or percent
  mass as a substitute for candidate-profile external error.
- Creating an NFRAQS carbon-weighting rule, endmember map, denominator, sample
  filter, or negative-contribution rule after observing NWSOWHL's fit result.
- Treating a species-selection contrast as a source-profile-choice contrast.
- Calling R3l, R3f, R3a, or R2 the R3nR7-AE baseline.
- Committing third-party raw reports or data to this public repository without
  independently verified redistribution rights.

## Editorial result

**NO-GO for a manuscript revision.** Retain PFFLS R4.3.30R3nR7-AE. Reopen the
external-validation claim only if a complete eight-object package is recovered
and independently reviewed.
